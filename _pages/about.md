**Odilon Linhares Carvalho Mendes** is a Ph.D. Candidate in Electrical Engineering at the **Federal University of Ceará (UFC)**. He holds a Master of Science in Aviation Safety and Continued Airworthiness from the **Aeronautics Institute of Technology (ITA)**, a Master of Science in Telecommunications Engineering from the **Federal Institute of Ceará (IFCE)**, and a Bachelor of Science in Mechatronics Engineering from the **Federal Institute of Ceará (IFCE)**. He completed an undergraduate academic exchange at **Arizona State University (ASU)**, as well as English language and American culture coursework at the **State University of New York at New Paltz (SUNY New Paltz)** and **Fulton-Montgomery Community College**. Currently, he is a **Researcher** at **Instituto Gridtech de Pesquisa e Desenvolvimento**.

---

### Research Areas and Interests

* **Smart Grid and Forecasting:** High-spatial-resolution, rapid-update mesoscale weather forecasting for transmission corridors, spanning remaining useful life (RUL) estimation algorithms, dynamic thermal rating, and georeferenced risk maps.
* **Control Systems and Automation:** Predictive control with transport delay (dead-time systems), modeling and control of MIMO systems, and tuning of robust and fractional-order controllers.
* **Optimization and Swarm Intelligence:** Application of swarm intelligence algorithms to solve complex engineering problems.
* **Computer Vision and Digital Image Processing:** Automated inspection methods, signal and image processing, and machine learning.
* **Biomedical Engineering:** Segmentation of anatomical structures and pathological detection in medical images (such as Optical Coherence Tomography - OCT).
* **Robotics:** Forward/inverse kinematics, actuation, instrumentation, and command systems for robotic manipulators.

### Áreas de Pesquisa e Interesses

* **Smart Grid e Previsão:** Previsão meteorológica de mesoescala em alta resolução espacial e atualização rápida para corredores de transmissão a algoritmos de estimativa de vida útil remanescente, capacidade térmica dinâmica e mapas de risco georreferenciados.
* **Sistemas de Controle e Automação:** Controle preditivo com atraso de transporte, modelagem e controle de sistemas MIMO, sintonia de controladores robustos e fracionários.
* **Otimização e Inteligência Coletiva:** Aplicação de algoritmos de inteligência de enxame (*Swarm Intelligence*) para resolução de problemas complexos de engenharia.
* **Visão Computacional e Processamento Digital de Imagens:** Métodos de inspeção automatizada, processamento de sinais e imagens e *machine learning*.
* **Engenharia Biomédica:** Segmentação de estruturas anatômicas e detecção patológica em imagens médicas (como Tomografia de Coerência Óptica - OCT).
* **Robótica:** Cinemática direta/inversa, acionamento, instrumentação e sistemas de comando para manipuladores robóticos.
