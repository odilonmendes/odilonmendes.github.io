### Project Overview

Dust and soiling deposition on the surface of photovoltaic modules drastically reduces the optical transmittance of the protective glass, severely degrading power generation efficiency in solar power plants.

This project investigates and develops advanced Digital Image Processing (DIP) and computer vision techniques for automatic detection, segmentation, and quantitative estimation of dust and defects on photovoltaic panel surfaces. The methodologies span capture via visible-light (RGB) and thermographic sensors mounted on Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), lowering inspection costs and enabling predictive cleaning schedules.

### Key Topics & Technologies
* **Digital Image Processing:** Spatial filtering, contrast enhancement, morphological transforms, and segmentation via thresholding and active contours.
* **UAV Inspection:** Automated collection of georeferenced aerial imagery.
* **Tools:** Python, OpenCV, NumPy, SciPy, MATLAB.
