### Project Overview

A Macular Hole (MH) is a full-thickness anatomical discontinuity in the retinal fovea that can lead to progressive loss of central visual acuity. The clinical gold standard for diagnosing and monitoring this condition is Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT), a non-invasive micrometer-scale cross-sectional imaging technique.

In this biomedical engineering and computer vision project, an automated methodology was developed to accurately detect and segment the boundaries and geometry of macular holes in OCT B-scan images. The algorithms overcome inherent speckle noise and illumination variations, providing ophthalmologists with quantitative volume, minimum diameter, and base diameter metrics essential for surgical planning and post-vitrectomy prognosis.

### Technical Highlights
* **Robust Segmentation:** Adaptive preprocessing, speckle noise attenuation, foveal localization, and refined morphological segmentation.
* **Clinical Validation:** Experimental validation against manual annotations by specialist ophthalmologists, achieving high sensitivity, specificity, and Dice/Jaccard similarity coefficients.
* **Featured Publication:** Published in the prestigious international journal **IEEE Access**.
