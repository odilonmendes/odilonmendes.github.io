### Abstract

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs / drones) have exponentially expanded their civilian and military application domains. Integrating embedded computer vision systems empowers these aircraft to perform complex autonomous operations, including visual odometry navigation, obstacle avoidance, target tracking, and geospatial mapping. This paper presents a systematic review of computational architectures, optical sensors, and vision algorithms applied to UAVs.
