### Abstract

A comprehensive review of image processing and artificial intelligence approaches proposed in the literature for the automatic segmentation of macular holes (MH) from Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) exams. The article discusses intrinsic biomedical imaging challenges (speckle noise, anatomical variations, low signal-to-noise ratio) and compares classical segmentation methods with deep learning techniques.

* **Journal:** Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Systems, 1(1), 2020.
* **Keywords:** Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT), Macular Hole, Deep Learning, Image Segmentation.
