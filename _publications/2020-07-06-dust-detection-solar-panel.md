### Abstract

Dust and soiling deposition on photovoltaic module surfaces is one of the primary factors degrading solar power generation efficiency. This review analyzes the state of the art in digital image processing and computer vision techniques applied to automated detection and quantification of soiling on solar panels, highlighting capture methodologies (RGB, thermal infrared, and UAV aerial imagery) alongside segmentation and classification algorithms.
