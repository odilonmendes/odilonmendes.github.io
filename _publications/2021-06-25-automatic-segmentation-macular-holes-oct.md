### Resumo (Abstract)

Macular hole (MH) is a retinal disease characterized by a full-thickness anatomical defect in the fovea, leading to visual impairment. Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) is the gold standard diagnostic tool for visualizing and quantifying retinal layers and macular conditions. This work presents an automated framework for the segmentation and quantitative evaluation of macular holes in OCT B-scans, aiming to assist ophthalmologists in diagnosis, surgical planning, and post-operative monitoring.
