### Abstract

This work addresses the design and optimal tuning of fractional-order PID (FOPID) controllers applied to a multivariable (MIMO) aircraft model. The multi-objective tuning problem of fractional parameters and controller gains is formulated and solved using Swarm Intelligence algorithms (PSO), ensuring robust stability, reference tracking, and dynamic decoupling across flight control axes.

* **Conference:** XXIV Brazilian Automatic Control Conference (CBA 2022)
* **Area:** Aircraft Control Systems, Multivariable Control, Swarm Intelligence.
