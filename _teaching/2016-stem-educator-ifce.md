Volunteered as an instructor for a community-focused extension initiative at IFCE, delivering comprehensive pre-university lectures in geography to underserved students in Fortaleza.

* Translated complex technical and scientific concepts into accessible, engaging lessons, empowering students to achieve higher academic performance and gain admission to higher education institutions.
* Fostered an inclusive learning environment and developed leadership, public speaking, and community-building skills while mentoring young talent in STEM fields.
